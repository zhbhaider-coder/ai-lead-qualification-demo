How scoring works

Every lead is scored with the same transparent, deterministic model — no randomness, no black box. Each answer maps to a 0–100 subscore per factor, and the total is the weighted sum, rounded to a whole number. Hot is 75+, Warm is 50–74, Cold is below 50.

Budget · 25% Authority · 20% Need · 25% Timeline · 15% Engagement · 15%

Score = 0.25 × Budget + 0.20 × Authority + 0.25 × Need + 0.15 × Timeline + 0.15 × Engagement
